# Night sky v1

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. PNG is the original; WebP is the homepage delivery asset (quality 88). Decorative, static artwork displayed only in dark mode.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: static night-sky artwork for the right-hand portrait frame of a minimalist academic personal homepage. Generate only the artwork, not a webpage mockup. Portrait composition roughly 4:5. Almost-black, very desaturated midnight navy sky, quietly scattered tiny fine white and cool grey-blue stars, organic uneven density with generous dark breathing room. A few slightly brighter pinpoint stars, restrained natural atmospheric depth, beautifully subtle and contemplative. Uniformly dark edges to sit beside charcoal #151615 and warm off-white serif typography. No prominent Milky Way band, no colorful nebula, no purple, no planets, no moon, no landscape or horizon, no constellation lines, no large lens flares, no text, no border, no watermark. Keep it visually quiet, not a dramatic sci-fi wallpaper. Crisp small stars rather than blurry bokeh.
